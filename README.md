HEADWATERS — hold the valley, mind the water. A water-routing management game: open and shut 6 sluice gates (click or keys 1–6) to route the mountain spring through a glowing channel network to the reservoir, the mill, the wheat fields and 4 towns. Water only flows where you open it. 7 timed days, weather fronts on a forecast list (rain, storm surge, dry spell, snowmelt), beaver dams and log jams to clear, an upgrade draft between days (pick 1 of 3 from 10), and a boss on Day 7 — THE HUNDRED-YEAR FLOOD in 3 phases. WIN: survive Day 7 with at least 1 town standing. LOSE: the dam breaks, or every town runs dry. Vanilla canvas, no libraries, no network; procedural WebAudio (water rush, gate clunks, chimes, storm rumble).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Click a gate (or press 1–6) to open / shut it, click a beaver dam to clear it, P pauses, M mutes, Enter starts and restarts.
